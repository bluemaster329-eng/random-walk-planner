Why this works the way it does. Each simulated year draws a random return from a normal distribution centred on your expected return, with spread set by your volatility. That single assumption — that returns are random draws rather than a knowable number — is the spine of Malkiel's book. The wide fan you see is not a bug; it is the honest picture of an uncertain future.
