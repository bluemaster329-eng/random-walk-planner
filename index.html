Underlying Principle Each simulated year draws on a random return, from a normal distribution of expected returns, with the standard deviation altering based on the volatility settings. The assumption that this is underpinned by is that returns are random rather than a knowable number, which is the key thesis of Malkiel's book. The wide fan represents the uncertainty of future returns.
